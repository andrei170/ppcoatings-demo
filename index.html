Read this first — what changed, and what deliberately did not

Nothing here is a new website. Your site is well built, fast on a phone, and your 571 products are already catalogued across 27 categories. That is the expensive part and it is done. This demonstration only switches things on.

What changed: trade prices and an add-to-basket on tools and consumables; an “order it again” list from a contractor's own history; a page built for specifiers around the CPD presentations and 25 brochures you already have; and your Royal Hillsborough Castle work moved to the front and given a real web address.

What deliberately did not change: coatings are still an enquiry. Jotun, Remmers and Flexcrete systems need a substrate, a specification and a conversation with your team, and nothing here tries to sell them from a dropdown. The tools and consumables are the ones already sitting on your site with no price on them.

Prices, stock levels, account names and order history above are invented placeholders. We do not know your trade prices and have not guessed at your margins. If your prices are agreed account by account — which for a distributor they usually are — then the right build is exactly what is shown here: a login, not public pricing.

571 products. Twenty-seven years. One phone.

You are not a contractor and you do not need adverts. You need the counter you have already built to be open when your customers are working.
